# PhoneSweep roadmap

Where PhoneSweep is going. Plans can change; ideas and votes are welcome on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/KimsFerdy/PhoneSweep/issues).

## v1.0 (released)

Scan, save and clean an Android phone from a Windows PC: storage map, verified backups (plus zip archives and restore), duplicate finder, junk sweep, photo/video compression, Nuke with Nuke Protector, app insights (risk flags, live CPU/RAM, battery use, background restrictions), Ask AI, Wi-Fi connection, and installers. See the [v1.0.0 release](https://github.com/KimsFerdy/PhoneSweep/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

## v1.1

- **Compare duplicates side by side:** click a duplicate group to see every copy next to each other: photos, video frames, music you can play right there, and text files rendered as text. Keep one copy, several, or all of them, and delete the rest right from that window.
- **Right-click and big preview in Files:** Preview (zoomable photos, music, text, ← → to browse), Open on PC, Save to PC, Mark as keep, Copy path, Delete permanently (following Nuke Protector).
- **Updates from inside the app:** Settings → Updates checks for new versions (also quietly once a day at startup, can be turned off), shows the release notes, downloads the right installer, checks it against GitHub's checksum, installs it and reopens PhoneSweep.

## v1.2 – v1.9: quality of life

The 1.x updates are about polish rather than big new features: smoother everyday use, faster scans and transfers, clearer wording, fixes for problems people report, and small improvements across the app. Each release lists what changed in its release notes.

If something annoys you or slows you down, [open an issue](https://github.com/KimsFerdy/PhoneSweep/issues). That's exactly what 1.x is for.

## v2.0: the big one

### Google Drive upload
Send your photos, videos, documents and music straight to Google Drive, not just to your PC. The same safety rules apply: a file counts as saved only once the upload is confirmed.

### Automatic Nuke queue for forgotten files
Files you haven't used for a period you choose (for example 6 months) are added to the Nuke queue automatically, ready for you to review. Nothing is deleted without you, and Nuke Protector still keeps anything that isn't backed up.

### PhoneSweep for Android and iPhone
A PhoneSweep app that runs on the phone itself, no PC needed. This will be a paid app on Google Play and the App Store. The Windows app stays free and open source.

### PC Sweep
The PhoneSweep approach applied to computers: see what fills your disk, find duplicates and junk, and clean up safely. For Windows, macOS and Linux.

### PhoneSweep Watcher
A companion that keeps an eye on your phone between clean-ups and tells you when something needs attention, so it never gets to "full" again.
